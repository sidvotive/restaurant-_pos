# Product Vision

## Vision

Build a modern cloud-based Restaurant Management System inspired by Petpooja, but with:

- A completely original UI/UX and branding.
- Modular architecture.
- Multi-tenant SaaS support.
- Mobile-first design.
- Offline-capable POS.
- AI-ready architecture.

## Target users

The platform serves a broad range of food-service business types:

- Restaurants
- Cafes
- QSR (Quick Service Restaurants)
- Fine Dining
- Food Courts
- Cloud Kitchens
- Bakery
- Sweet Shops
- Multi-chain Restaurants

## Design principles

| Principle            | What it means for us |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Mobile-first**     | Every operator-facing surface must work well on a tablet or phone before it works on a desktop. |
| **Offline-capable**  | The POS keeps taking orders and billing when the network drops, and reconciles when it returns. |
| **Modular**          | Modules (POS, Inventory, CRM, etc.) are independently ownable and deployable. |
| **Multi-tenant**     | One deployment serves many companies, brands, and branches with strict isolation. |
| **AI-ready**         | Data models and events are captured cleanly so forecasting, recommendations, and analytics can be layered on later. |
| **Original by design** | Petpooja-inspired in capability, but original in interface, branding, and interaction. |
